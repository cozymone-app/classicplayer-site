Privacy Policy

Effective: October 25, 2026

ClassicPlayer does not collect any data. We have no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your music and files

The app reads music and files on your device (your Music library and files you import) only to play them. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Internet radio

Radio stations are loaded from the public radio-browser.info directory, station streams are played directly from the stations, and station icons are loaded through the Google favicon service. Like any web request, these services receive your device's IP address. ClassicPlayer does not send them any other personal information.

Settings

Your settings, favorites and playlists are stored only on your device.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
