ClassicPlayer Support

ClassicPlayer is a classic click-wheel music player for iPhone and Android. It plays music stored on your device, files you import (folders, flash drives, Files app) and internet radio.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, ideas: 12cozyhome@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

FAQ

How do I add music? Use Settings → Import Music to import files or folders, or allow access to your Music library.

Radio station is not playing. Stations come from the public radio-browser.info directory and may go offline; try another station.
